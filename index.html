Our Story

Born From A Love Of The Ocean

AquaRush was founded in 2017 by a group of water sports enthusiasts who wanted to share the magic of the Tamil Nadu coastline with the world.

From a single surfboard rental to a full-service water sports hub, we've grown into Tamil Nadu's most trusted adventure destination — all while keeping safety and fun at the heart of everything we do.
